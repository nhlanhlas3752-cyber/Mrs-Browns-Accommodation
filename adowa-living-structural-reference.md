# Adowa Living — Structural & Visual Reference

This document is a structural/visual reference for the Adowa Living website, intended to brief a redesign/restructure. It describes layout, sections, and content organization in plain language (not verbatim copy or source code).

## Shared Site Framework

- **Header:** Sticky white header on every page. Adowa Living logo (left). Horizontal nav: About, Residences, FAQs, Contact. Two CTA buttons on the right: an outlined/filled "Login" button and a solid lime-green "Apply" button.
- **Footer:** Dark brown background containing a "Get in touch" contact form (Full Name, Email Address, Message fields, green Submit button), social media icon links (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, WhatsApp), a Privacy Policy link, copyright line, and a "Website by [agency]" credit.
- **Recurring teaser banners:** An FAQ teaser ("Have a question? Dive into our FAQs...") and various "Apply now" CTA banners appear near the bottom of most pages before the footer.

## Home Page

1. Full-bleed hero photo with orange eyebrow label, large bold headline, subheading paragraph, and lime-green rounded "Apply now" CTA button.
2. "Find your room" section: horizontally scrollable carousel of residence cards. Each card = photo, location pill tag, property name, price line, email, phone number.
3. Alternating full-width photo/text banner sections covering community life and lifestyle imagery.
4. "Facilities for days" amenities section: icon-grid tiles (icon + label) for things like transport, TV rooms, study areas, parking, laundry, security, kitchen, Wi-Fi, cleaning services, biometric access.
5. FAQ teaser banner with heading and CTA.
6. "Let's connect" social section: heading, paragraph, row of social icon links, followed by a horizontally scrollable photo carousel of community/event images.
7. Footer contact form (shared template).

## About Page

1. Hero banner (photo + eyebrow label + headline + paragraph + CTA), same pattern as Home.
2. "Find your community" text block with paragraph and Apply CTA.
3. Two-image side-by-side gallery/carousel section.
4. FAQ teaser banner with an embedded search bar.
5. "Our vision" text block section.
6. Photo banner section ("Chill areas") highlighting recreational/common spaces.
7. Closing "Find a room" CTA block.
8. Footer (shared template).

## Residences Page (listing/index)

1. Hero banner (same pattern as other pages).
2. Filter bar: pill-style buttons for regions (Gauteng, Kwa-Zulu Natal, Eastern Cape, Western Cape).
3. Single-column stacked list of property cards. Each card = photo, location tag, property name, price/status line (or "Opening [year]" / "Under Construction" for future properties), contact email/phone where applicable.
4. "Apply now" banner.
5. FAQ teaser banner.
6. Footer (shared template).

## Individual Residence Listing Page (e.g., Ellis Park Student Village)

1. Hero photo with a rounded white overlay "card" containing: location tag, property name (large heading), descriptive paragraph about the residence, and an "Apply now" button.
2. Facilities/amenities icon-grid section (same visual pattern as Home page, scoped to this property — kitchen, laundry, parking, study rooms, TV rooms, etc.).
3. Two-column masonry-style photo gallery: numerous exterior and interior photos (common areas, kitchens, bedrooms, building exteriors), no captions.
4. Embedded Google Map pinned to the property's location with nearby points of interest labeled.
5. FAQ teaser banner.
6. Footer contact form (shared template).

## FAQs Page

1. Large heading ("What do you need help with?") with a subheading.
2. Search bar with input field and a green "Search" button.
3. Two side-by-side filter columns: "Select your category" (list of topic links e.g. Amenities, House Rules, Documentation, Funding, NSFAS, property-specific categories, General Information, Applications) and "Select your language" (e.g., English).
4. Long accordion-style list of expandable question rows (plus-icon + question text; answers hidden until expanded).
5. Footer (shared template).

## Apply / Register Portal

- **Note:** This page is hosted on a separate third-party domain/portal (a tenant management system, credited as "Powered by Modus10" in the footer), not the main custom-built marketing site.
- Layout: Adowa Living logo beside a plain "REGISTER" heading at the top.
- Long single-column form organized into labeled sections down the left margin, paired with input fields on the right:
  - **Personal Information:** first/middle/last name, nationality dropdown.
  - **Identity:** SA ID number toggle, age, date of birth, gender toggle (Male/Female), disability yes/no toggle.
  - **Contact Details:** email address, primary contact number.
  - **Building Group:** radio selection between available residence properties (e.g., Frederick Street, Ellis Park Student Village).
  - **Terms:** two required checkboxes (accept Terms and Conditions; consent to processing personal information for credit report purposes).
  - **Notice:** a note that starting the application cancels other pending application invites.
- Closing orange "Submit" button, with a "Powered by Modus10" credit and copyright line beneath it.
